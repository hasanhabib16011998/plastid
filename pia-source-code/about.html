Plastid Interior and Architecture
was Founded in 2022 in Dhaka.

Plastid Interior and Architecture was founded with a singular goal — to transform everyday spaces into deeply personal experiences. What began as a passion for aesthetic excellence has grown into a full-fledged design studio committed to creativity, craftsmanship, and client-centric innovation.
